About

The Engineer
Behind The Sound

At 21 years old, Colin Ishe Makoni Jr. is one of the youngest engineers operating at the highest tier of live production on the African continent — and the trajectory of his career makes every year count double.

His foundation was forged at Events Evolution, Zimbabwe's premier live production company, where he trained alongside some of the continent's finest engineers and technicians — including mentors Tatenda Rushwaya, Blessing Bero, and Endelani Kachepa. That environment didn't just teach technique; it built instinct.

Specialising in monitor engineering, Colin has the rare ability to create a perfect on-stage world for every artist — translating the chaos of a live stage into a controlled, personal sonic environment that lets performers do what they do best. From intimate in-ears to complex multi-wedge rigs, his console of choice is the Allen & Heath range.

In just over two years, Colin has been in the room — and on the stage — with some of the biggest names in Afro, Gospel, and Secular music globally. For most of these artists, his role was as their personal production technician: managing their backline, liaising on technical requirements, and ensuring everything around the performer was dialled in before the first note hit. Work that took him to Botswana, Zambia, and beyond. In April 2026, he stepped into full freelance operation: prepared for tours, festivals, and headline productions worldwide.

"Remember, when doing monitors — it's all about what the artist wants."

— Martyaudio · Colin's mentor & hero in audio
